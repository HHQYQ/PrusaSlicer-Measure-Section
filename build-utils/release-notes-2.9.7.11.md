PrusaSlicer Measure & Section 2.9.7.11 adds chamfers and fillets to supported STL edges in the Windows FFF editor.

- Click a highlighted straight edge or a complete cylindrical hole rim. Use two or three visible points to disambiguate nearby edges.
- Preview the requested size in millimeters. The tool blocks cuts that conflict with one another or reach protected surfaces, and flags walls that may need a slicing check.
- Chamfer or fillet the straight perimeter of a planar face in one batch where the cuts can meet at miters. Rectangular perimeters use a single cutter when the mesh supports it. The preview highlights cut surfaces in orange.
- Apply a batch as one undoable operation. Save the resulting mesh in a 3MF project or export it as STL.

The editor also supports view transparency for a whole part, selected bodies, and individual surface regions. Set transparency from 0% to 100%, pick through transparent surfaces, and retain the display settings in a 3MF project.
